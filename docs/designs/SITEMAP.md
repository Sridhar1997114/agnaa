## 2. Document Lifecycle

Each specification progresses through three formal EGM phases:
1. **Draft / Brainstorm:** Concept explored and clarified by the Elephant with open-ended problem statement drilling.
2. **Goldfish Review:** Zero-context subagent swarms evaluate the document across 6 lenses (contrarian/YAGNI, cost/economics, ergonomics, migration, observability, tech debt).
3. **Approved / Active:** Owner approves trade-offs; implementation strictly follows the verified specification.
