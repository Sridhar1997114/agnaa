## 2. Statutory Planning & Byelaw Rigor

Every AGNAA spatial commission is designed from day one to achieve 100% statutory clearance under Telangana and national codes:

### 2.1 Telangana Building Rules (G.O. Ms. No. 168 & TG-bPASS 2026)
* **Single-Window Online Sanction (TG-bPASS):** Self-certification for plots up to 500 sq.m; single-window processing for plots exceeding 500 sq.m and commercial high-rises.
* **Front Setback / Road-Side Margin:** Regulated by abutting road width.
  * Road width < 12m: 3.0m minimum front setback.
  * Road width 12m to 18m: 4.0m minimum front setback.
  * Road width > 18m: 6.0m minimum front setback.
* **Side and Rear Setbacks:** Function of building height ($H$) and plot depth. For residential villas with height $\le 10\text{m}$, minimum 1.5m side and 2.0m rear setbacks are maintained for natural light and fire access.
* **Lake Protection & FTL Boundaries:** Strict adherence to Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (HMWSSB) and Lake Protection Committee buffer zones:
  * 30m buffer from Full Tank Level (FTL) for notified lakes (Osman Sagar, Himayat Sagar, Durgam Cheruvu).
  * 9m buffer along major nala drainage courses.

### 2.2 National Building Code of India (NBC 2026, SP 7:2026)
* **Clause 12.2 (Habitable Rooms):**
  * Minimum single habitable room carpet area: $9.5\text{ m}^2$ with minimum clear width of $2.4\text{m}$.
  * Minimum clear ceiling height: $2.75\text{m}$ ($9\text{ ft}$) from finished floor level to ceiling soffit. Permissible relaxation to $2.4\text{m}$ only for mechanically air-conditioned zones.
  * Natural ventilation aperture: Minimum clear opening area $\ge 10\%$ of room floor area.
* **Clause 12.4 (Building Height Coupling):** Ratio of maximum permissible building height to abutting road width ensuring adequate solar daylighting angle ($\ge 45^\circ$).

### 2.3 Structural Code Compliance (IS 456:2000 & IS 13920)
* All concrete mixes specified as design mixes ($M25$ to $M40$ minimum for RCC framed structures).
* Ductile detailing of reinforced concrete structures subjected to seismic forces (Zone II seismic compliance for Hyderabad Deccan plateau).

## 3. The Turnkey Material Escrow Model

Conventional construction in Hyderabad suffers from contractor markups (typically 15–25% hidden on materials) and substandard substitutions. AGNAA operates a **Zero-Markup Material Escrow System**:

```
Client Escrow Deposit
       │
       ▼
AGNAA Verified Stage-Gate Release
       │
       ├─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
       ▼                         ▼                         ▼
Factory Direct Steel      Batch Plant Concrete       Vetted Master Labor
(Tata Tiscon / JSW)       (UltraTech M25/M30)        (AGNAA Pros Guild)
Zero Dealer Markup        Cylinder Compression Tests Stage-Gate Verification
```

### Key Pillars of the Escrow Contract:
1. **Direct Mill Procurement:**
   - Primary TMT Steel (Fe 500D) purchased directly from authorized distributors (Tata Tiscon, JSW Neosteel) at wholesale spot rates.
   - Cement procured directly from UltraTech / Bharathi batching networks.
   - Original manufacturer invoices and test certificates (MTC) shared directly with the client docket.
2. **Stage-Gate Milestone Disbursements:**
   - *Milestone 01:* Sub-structure & Plinth Level casting (15%).
   - *Milestone 02:* Ground & First Floor RCC Frame completion (25%).
   - *Milestone 03:* Brickwork masonry & plumbing/electrical rough-in (20%).
   - *Milestone 04:* External plastering, weatherproofing & fenestrations (15%).
   - *Milestone 05:* Internal marble/granite flooring & false ceiling (15%).
   - *Milestone 06:* Joinery, lighting fixtures, snag rectification & handover (10%).

## 4. The 4-Stage Architectural Continuum

1. **Phase 01: Algorithmic Byelaw Zoning**
   - Survey verification, total station boundary mapping, GHMC G.O. 168 envelope optimization, and TG-bPASS drawing sanction set.
2. **Phase 02: Spatial Physics & BIM**
   - Passive solar orientation (Deccan sun path analysis), wind-catchers, thermal mass placement, and full structural 3D BIM coordination.
3. **Phase 03: Turnkey Construction Execution**
   - Daily site supervisor logs, material batch testing, stage-gate inspections, and digital client portal progress updates.
4. **Phase 04: Micro-Architecture & Joinery Handover**
   - Solid Burma teak millwork, brass architectural hardware, acoustic panelling, and final occupancy certificate procurement.
